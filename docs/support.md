# Support

Click-Dog has two support paths: public community support for the open-source
project, and commercial support for teams that want direct help with production
deployment or integration work.

## Community Support

Use GitHub for project questions, bug reports, and feature requests:

- Search existing [issues](https://github.com/coltconsulting/click-dog/issues)
  before opening a new one.
- Include the Click-Dog version, deployment method, relevant config snippets
  with secrets removed, and any `click-dog check` output that applies.
- For suspected security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting path in
  [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/coltconsulting/click-dog/blob/master/SECURITY.md)
  instead of a public issue.

Community support is best effort and does not include response-time guarantees.

## Commercial Support

Email support is available for production deployment and integration enquiries.

Support enquiries can cover:

- Production setup questions
- Configuration review
- ClickHouse span-log and query-log readiness
- OTEL collector and exporter setup
- Datadog, Honeycomb, Grafana Tempo, and generic OTLP integration
- Upgrade planning
- Troubleshooting export, dashboard, or query-analysis behavior

For support enquiries, email
[support@click-dog.com](mailto:support@click-dog.com).

## Custom Development

Custom development is available for teams that need Click-Dog adapted to their
environment.

Common examples include:

- New exporter targets or collector patterns
- Deployment automation for internal platforms
- Dashboard, monitor, or alert templates
- Additional filtering, enrichment, or reporting behavior
- Production hardening and operational review
- Integration with existing observability tooling

## Engagements

Commercial support and custom development are quoted based on the scope,
urgency, and shape of the work.

Email [support@click-dog.com](mailto:support@click-dog.com) with a short
description of what you need and the environment you are running.
